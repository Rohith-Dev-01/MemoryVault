Six Thoughtful Pillars

Everything your loved ones need, arranged with clarity.

Prepare an unambiguous guide for your digital footprint while keeping every credential secure and every farewell letter private until verified.

01

Digital Asset Registry

Record email accounts, cloud photo libraries, social profiles, and recurring subscriptions using account identifiers only—never passwords.

02

Explicit Final Wishes

Assign clear instructions to each asset: preserve the account, archive selected memories, request memorialization, or close via official provider channels.

03

Trusted Contacts

Gentle Wellness Monitoring

A measured, four-stage inactivity protocol.

Every sign-in or one-click dashboard check-in resets your timer to Day 0. If extended silence occurs, reminders escalate thoughtfully before notifying your trusted circle.

Day 30

First Reminder

A gentle check-in email is dispatched directly to your registered address. No contacts are alerted.

Day 35

Second Reminder

A follow-up reminder asks you to sign in or confirm activity so your legacy timer resets cleanly.

Day 40

Final Reminder

A final five-day notice informs you that trusted contacts will be asked to check in if silence continues.

Day 45

Trusted Contact Wellness Alert

Trusted contacts are asked to check on your well-being. Zero private vault contents are ever emailed.

“Inactivity is a signal to check on someone you care about—never an automatic key to their private life.”
